
The **SC-01 Surface-Cap Repair System** is a one-handed, rapid-deployment spot-repair puck engineered for pressurized pipe breaches. Designed for emergency utility maintenance and quick-response infrastructure triage, it combines a positive ratcheting lock with a moisture-curing polyurethane grout injection to achieve immediate mechanical sealing under operational pressures.

## Core Architecture & Components
* **Telescoping 316 Stainless Struts:** High-corrosion-resistance structural members providing adjustable radial expansion to bridge variable pipe diameters and secure the sealing face.
* **Positive Ratcheting Lock:** Mechanical engagement mechanism ensuring the clamping pressure remains locked in place without slipping under line vibration or pressure spikes.
* **Moisture-Curing Polyurethane Grout (MDI-based):** Reactive sealing compound designed to cure rapidly in wet environments, forming a resilient, chemically bonded barrier over the breach.
* **One-Handed Deployment Ergonomics:** Compact puck geometry allowing single-operator placement and actuation in confined trench or overhead environments.

## Technical Specifications
* **Holding Force:** Engineered for a minimum of 400 N clamping and retention force across the sealing interface.
* **Material Composition:** 316 stainless steel structural components with high-grade elastomer backing and MDI polyurethane formulation.
* **Operating Environment:** Submerged, damp, or pressurized pipe surfaces up to standard municipal line ratings.
* **Deployment Time:** Rapid actuation sequence designed for sub-minute mechanical lock-in.

## Engineering Objectives & Test Matrix
1. **Holding Force Verification:** Stress-test the ratcheting lock and telescoping 316 struts against simulated internal pipe pressure to ensure structural integrity above the 400 N target.
2. **Grout Cure Kinetics:** Evaluate MDI-based polyurethane expansion and bond strength under continuous moisture exposure.
3. **Ergonomic Deployment Trials:** Assess one-handed actuation ease in simulated restricted-access trench spaces.
